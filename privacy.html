Privacy Policy

How Rivet Software (Pty) Ltd collects, uses, discloses and safeguards information when you use the Rivet platform.

1. Introduction

Rivet Software (Pty) Ltd ("Rivet", "we", "us", or "our") operates the Rivet platform, a WhatsApp-first business automation solution for small and medium-sized businesses across Africa. By using Rivet, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

2. Information we collect

We may collect account registration details, business and billing information, WhatsApp Business Account credentials and authorisation tokens obtained through Meta's approved onboarding, business configuration data, automation flows, templates, catalogue information and communications with our team.

We also collect operational information such as pages and features used, browser and device information, IP address, access times, error logs, API metadata, cookies and similar session technologies. With your authorisation we receive relevant WhatsApp Business Account data from Meta and integration data from connected platforms such as Shopify or WooCommerce.

3. How we use information

We use information to provide and improve Rivet, process subscriptions, send service and security communications, respond to support requests, analyse platform usage, prevent fraud and technical abuse, enforce our terms and comply with legal obligations. Marketing communications are sent only where permitted, and you may withdraw consent at any time.

4. WhatsApp and Meta data

Rivet integrates with the WhatsApp Business Platform through Meta's official APIs. When you connect an account, you authorise Rivet to access the relevant WABA and phone-number identifiers, send and receive WhatsApp messages according to your configurations, and process delivery and read status information for operational purposes.

We do not sell WhatsApp message content or contact data. Message content is processed to provide the automation and inbox services configured by the business.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share data with service providers that help deliver hosting, payments, analytics and related infrastructure; with Meta as required for WhatsApp Business services; with legal authorities where required by law; or with a successor entity in a legitimate corporate transaction subject to applicable safeguards.

6. Data retention

We keep personal information for as long as an account is active or as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations. Message logs and automation data use a default retention period of 90 days unless a different supported retention configuration applies. Following account termination, data is deleted or anonymised within 30 days where possible, subject to legal retention requirements.

7. Data security

Rivet uses technical and organisational safeguards including workspace isolation, access controls, encrypted secrets, audit logging and controlled message-processing infrastructure. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but we continually work to reduce operational and security risk.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including South Africa's POPIA where relevant, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, object to certain processing, or request information about how your data is handled. Contact us at legal@rivetsoftware.dev.

9. Cookies

Rivet uses cookies and similar technologies for essential sessions, security and service analytics. We do not use customer WhatsApp message content for behavioural advertising.

10. International transfers

Some infrastructure and service providers may process information outside South Africa. Where this occurs, Rivet uses appropriate contractual and technical safeguards consistent with applicable data-protection requirements.

11. Children's privacy

Rivet is a business platform and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children for the purpose of creating Rivet business accounts.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect product, legal or operational changes. Material changes will be communicated through the platform, website or other appropriate channel.

13. Contact us

Privacy questions can be sent to legal@rivetsoftware.dev. Rivet Software (Pty) Ltd operates from South Africa.
