ABOUT RIVET

Built in Africa.
Built for Africa.

Rivet is a WhatsApp business automation platform built for SMBs across Africa, starting in South Africa. We help businesses automate customer communication without losing the human touch.

WHY WE BUILT RIVET

WhatsApp is where business already happens.

For many African businesses, WhatsApp is not an extra support channel. It is where bookings, orders, follow-ups and customer relationships happen every day.

Rivet gives those businesses a direct connection to Meta's official Cloud API, automation they can manage without code, and local integrations without adding a middleman between the business and its customers.

HOW RIVET OPERATES

Direct, transparent and built for control.

META

Official Cloud API

Businesses connect their own WhatsApp Business accounts directly through Meta's official platform.

0%

No messaging markup

WhatsApp messaging fees remain visible and controlled by the business, without Rivet hiding them inside a bundled rate.

ZA

Built in South Africa

Designed around African business workflows, local commerce and the realities of running customer operations here.

WHATSAPP USAGE

What Rivet does on WhatsApp.

Transactional notifications

Order confirmations, appointment reminders, delivery updates and payment receipts for customers who have opted in.

Automated conversation flows

Inbound flows for bookings, lead qualification, FAQs, support routing and structured customer journeys.

Agent handoff

Human teams can take over from automation in the shared inbox while keeping the conversation history intact.

Rivet does not send unsolicited bulk messages and is designed to operate within Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform rules and approved template requirements.
