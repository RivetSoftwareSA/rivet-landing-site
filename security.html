SECURITY

Built to keep
businesses separated.

Rivet is designed around workspace isolation, controlled access, encrypted credentials and an auditable message pipeline.

01

Workspace isolation

Business data is scoped to its workspace so one tenant cannot read or operate on another tenant's data.

02

Role-based access

Owners, administrators, agents and viewers receive only the permissions appropriate to their role.

03

Encrypted secrets

WhatsApp credentials and other sensitive integration secrets are protected at rest and are not exposed in normal application views.

04

Auditable operations

Key actions, publishes and message events are recorded so teams can reconstruct what happened and when.

05

Controlled delivery

Rate limiting, retries and delivery controls help protect message quality and reduce duplicate or runaway sends.

06

Data retention controls

Retention policies are designed to keep operational data only as long as it remains useful or legally required.

MESSAGE DELIVERY

Reliability starts before the send.

Rivet records a reply and its outbound work together before the message is released to the send pipeline. That gives retries something durable to work from and helps prevent duplicated messages.

  • Inbound webhook validation and controlled processing.
  • Transactional outbox for durable outbound work.
  • Retry-aware sending with operational logs.
  • Workspace-scoped access throughout the pipeline.
